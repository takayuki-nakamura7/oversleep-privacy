# Oversleep

Oversleep is a native iOS alarm app that combines your normal wake schedule with
your calendar, so an early meeting can move tomorrow's alarm earlier — without
you having to remember to set one.

It is built around a single promise: **you should always be able to tell what
time you will be woken, and why.**

## What it does

- Keeps a recurring wake schedule, one time per weekday.
- Reads the calendars you choose and moves the alarm earlier when an event needs
  it, using a buffer you control.
- Explains every wake time in terms of the one thing that decided it.
- Shows tomorrow's alarm on the Home Screen, including when it differs from your
  routine.
- Lets you skip any upcoming day without changing your recurring schedule.

## Calendars

Oversleep reads Apple Calendar on the device, and can optionally connect
directly to Google Calendar. Access is read-only in both cases: Oversleep never
creates, edits, or deletes calendar data, and it reads only the calendars you
select.

## Privacy

Oversleep has no backend, no analytics, and no advertising. Calendar events are
processed on the device.
